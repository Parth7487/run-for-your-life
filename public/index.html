RULE 01

            

              
Strictly On Foot (No Vehicles)

              
All movement must be on foot (walking, jogging, sprinting). Cars, bicycles, e-scooters, skateboards, rollerblades, and public transport are strictly forbidden. Infractions result in immediate disqualification.

            

          


          

            
RULE 02

            

              
Perimeter Boundary Integrity

              
Operatives must remain within the circular arena zone set by the host at all times. Stepping out of bounds triggers a flashing red warning and siren. Runners outside the zone for more than 30 seconds forfeit the round.

            

          


          

            
RULE 03

            

              
Public Access & Safety First

              
No trespassing on private property, construction zones, or locked buildings. Stay in public parks, sidewalks, and open outdoor spaces. Always obey pedestrian signals and watch for vehicular traffic.

            

          


          

            
RULE 04

            

              
Gentle Physical Tagging

              
A tag must be a physical touch with an arm's reach (gentle tap on shoulder, arm, or back). No tackling, grabbing, holding, or physical roughness. Once tagged, the runner confirms in-app and switches to Spectator mode.

            

          


          

            
RULE 05

            

              
90-Second Disconnection Buffer

              
If network connection drops or phone battery dies temporarily, the game holds your operative identity for 90 seconds. Simply re-open the app using the exact same codename to instantly restore your position and role.

            

          


          

            
RULE 06

            

              
Tactical Chat & Photo Intel

              
Operatives may use the built-in tactical chat to share intelligence and snap live camera photos of hiding spots or landmarks. Hunters can coordinate pincer movements, while runners can warn teammates.
