RULE 01

Strictly On Foot (No Vehicles)

All movement must be on foot (walking, jogging, sprinting). Cars, bicycles, e-scooters, skateboards, rollerblades, and public transit are strictly forbidden. Infractions result in immediate disqualification.

RULE 02

Perimeter Boundary Integrity (60s Red Alert Countdown)

Operatives must remain within the circular arena zone set by the host. Stepping outside triggers an intense pulsing RED ALERT SCREEN and an audible siren with a 60-second countdown. If an operative does not return inside the perimeter within 60s, they are eliminated and infected by the toxic wasteland!

RULE 03

Live GPS Radar & Tactical Colors

All operatives stream live, uninterrupted GPS coordinates on the tactical map without lag or ping interval delays:
• 🏃 Blue Dots (#2563eb): Survivors running and evading.
• 💀 Red Dots (#ec3013): Zombies hunting and closing in.

RULE 04

5-Meter Chase Infection Rule

A Zombie infects a Survivor by closing in and staying within a 5-meter radius for 10 continuous seconds. Survivors will see an on-screen evasion warning with a progress meter. Alternatively, a Zombie can physically scan the Survivor's personal QR tag code. Zombies CANNOT infect other Zombies!

RULE 05

15-Meter Zombie Proximity Beep Warning

When an active Zombie approaches within 15 meters of a Survivor, an urgent audio sonar beep triggers on the Survivor's phone, accompanied by an on-screen evasion warning banner. As the Zombie closes distance (15m → 10m → 5m), the beep frequency speeds up and intensifies, giving Survivors vital auditory warning to sprint away!

RULE 06

Infection Transformation

When a survivor is infected, their device vibrates, flashes red, and immediately transforms their UI into ZOMBIE MODE. Their blip turns RED, and they immediately join the horde to hunt down remaining survivors!

RULE 07

Tactical Sectors & Map Zones Explained

The tactical arena features 4 procedural landmarks:
• 🏛️ Arena Hub (Center): The central rally point (25m radius) where the game initiates.
• 🛡️ Sanctuary 1, 2, 3: Distributed shelters around the arena where survivors can regroup and take temporary cover behind physical landmarks.
• 📦 Supply Outpost: Key landmark stationed in the sector.
• 🚀 Extraction Zone Omega: The ultimate escape portal (30m radius)! Dormant during the main hunt, it unlocks during the final 20% of match time. Survivors who reach Extraction Omega escape and win the game!

RULE 08

90-Second Disconnection Buffer

If network connection drops or phone battery sleeps temporarily, the server holds your operative identity for 90 seconds. Simply re-open the app with the exact same codename to immediately restore your position and role.

RULE 09

Public Access & Safety First

No trespassing on private property, construction zones, or locked buildings. Stay in public parks, sidewalks, and open outdoor spaces. Always obey pedestrian signals and watch for vehicular traffic.
