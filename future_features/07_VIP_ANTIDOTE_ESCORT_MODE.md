# Feature 07: "VIP Antidote Escort" Game Mode

## 1. Executive Summary
An asymmetric team-based mode where 1 Survivor is designated as the **VIP Courier** carrying the synthetic Antidote. The entire match hinges on whether the VIP survives to the designated destination.

---

## 2. Gameplay Mechanics

### 1. VIP Designation & Role Assignment
- The VIP Courier is marked with a distinctive golden crown badge `👑 [VIP COURIER]`.
- The VIP's health/status is permanently displayed on all teammates' HUDs.

### 2. The Bio-Beacon Handicap
- Because the Antidote emits high-frequency radiation, the VIP's location pings on the Zombie radar every **20 seconds** (even during stealth).
- **The Team Strategy**: The other survivors must act as decoys, bodyguards, and scouts to draw zombies away from the VIP.

### 3. Victory / Loss Conditions
- **Survivor Victory**: If the VIP reaches the Safe Sanctuary Bunker intact, all survivors win a 3x point multiplier.
- **Zombie Victory**: If the VIP is tagged, the Antidote is destroyed and the match shifts to Sudden Death for all remaining players.
